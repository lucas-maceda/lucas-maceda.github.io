Hello, world

Why I am opening this portfolio and what I will write about here.

1 min read

I am opening this space to write in the first person about what I am working on and why. I build digital products and take them all the way to the customer: product, sales and engineering, with no middlemen.

Here I will write about what I learn building Motyo and Tracker Coach, about actually selling software — demos, conversations, closed deals — and about the technical decisions behind them.

There will also be room for my thesis: training computer-vision models on mammograms and testing whether what is learned in one domain carries over to another. If any of this sounds familiar, see you around soon.
