The problem

Many companies carry processes that live in spreadsheets, email and tools that don’t talk to each other. Digitising without judgement fixes nothing: it only adds chaos and one more tool that nobody uses.

What I did

Lublo is an IT consultancy for companies: audit, process digitalisation and custom software. The method is clear: first a two-to-three-week diagnosis that the client keeps even if they don’t continue; then one tool at a time, only when it will truly be used, with no per-user cost, no lock-in and exportable data. Everything is organised into five families: Sort, Measure, Automate, Expose and Protect.

Where it is

I handle the commercial side: understanding what hurts, naming it and proposing the shortest path to solve it, without promising more than can be delivered.
