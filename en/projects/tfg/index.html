A transferability study

Mammography classification and cross-domain transfer.

Role
Author
Year
2026 — 2027
Status
In progress
Tags
  • Deep Learning
  • Computer vision
  • Medicine
  • PyTorch

The question

Does a model trained on digitised mammograms (scanned film) work on direct digital mammograms, and the other way around? That is the question guiding my thesis: “Transferability of mammography classification models across acquisition domains (digitised vs. digital)”.

What I do

I train convolutional networks with PyTorch and timm on the university’s GPU server, using DICOM images and several seeds per experiment so the results are reproducible. I compare performance within each domain and when crossing from one to the other.

Where it is

The work runs from September 2026 to January 2027 and completes my BSc in Software Engineering at the EII in Oviedo. It is a personal research project: pure learning and a drive to measure things properly.
